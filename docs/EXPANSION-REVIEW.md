# Supporting cast and collection redesign

Reviewed 1 November 2026. There are 22 articles and 17 character-index entries, up from seven. No runtime dependency was added. Nothing was deployed or published.

## Coverage

Eight new articles: Paper King, Paper People, Lash, Chopsy the Pie, Coffin villain, Wolf villain, Cat villain, and Four-eyed bat villain. James and Bone Drones are cross-listed from their existing lore articles. Original routes, statuses, citations, search and article-only videos are preserved.

Paper King and Paper People story observations refer to [ZAMination’s official pilot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AFJtcP2cp0). Lash and Chopsy have community identification notes, rather than claims of primary-source naming. Four cameo titles are descriptive editorial labels. Unknown names, powers, species and backstories stay unconfirmed. Allie, Kazam and Sarge were excluded because their actual pilot appearances were not established by the reviewed material.

## Interface

The directory has compact still-image cards, four group filters, name/role search, A–Z sorting, live result counts and an empty state. The episode hub includes a composition of the existing Batch Z renders, six linked story chapters, release facts, cast links and related lore. The homepage adds a supporting-cast feature and illustrated story trails. Brief unknown sections use tighter spacing. Mobile navigation, keyboard search and reduced-motion support remain.
